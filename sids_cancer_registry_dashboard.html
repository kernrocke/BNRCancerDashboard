🌊 SIDS-Specific Design Considerations
This framework addresses the unique epidemiological challenges of Small Island Developing States: small denominators requiring careful rate-stabilisation, limited registry infrastructure, high out-migration affecting survival follow-up, and elevated proportions of non-communicable diseases. Each module is calibrated for these realities.

FORECAST 2023–2027
⚠️ SIDS Projection Caveat
For small island registries with fewer than 50 annual cases per site, negative binomial models may be unstable. The framework automatically falls back to a simple linear model when glm.nb() fails to converge. Prediction intervals should be interpreted cautiously — rate stabilisation using empirical Bayes smoothing is recommended for sparse strata.

Adapting for Your SIDS Registry
Configuration checklist for new islands
📁 Data Files to Replace
✅ cancer_YYYY_YYYY_[registry].csv — your incidence data
✅ cancer_death_YYYY_YYYY.csv — mortality records
✅ WPP.xlsx — download from UN WPP portal for your country
✅ [country]_[admin].shp — GADM or national GIS shapefile
✅ admin_population_by_age.csv — census age-band data
⚙️ Code Parameters to Update
✅ Incidence year range (e.g. 2015–2023)
✅ Mortality year range
✅ Shapefile join column (e.g. NAME_1, DISTRICT)
✅ Admin unit name in standardize_parish_names()
✅ Login credentials in user_base (sodium hash)
✅ Dashboard title & logo in UI header
